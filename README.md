## 🌟 Key Highlights

* **12 Modular Architecture Sections**: Cleanly structured into Home, About, Projects, Skills, Toolkit, Experience, Education, Achievements, Certifications, My Approach, Resume & CV, and Contact.
* **Smart Active Scroll Spy**: Precision scroll-tracking powered by `getBoundingClientRect` with fluid anchor transitions.
* **Curriculum Vitae System**: In-browser high-definition modal viewer with direct one-click PDF download utility.
* **Dual Curated Themes**:
  * **Obsidian & Champagne (Dark)**: `#101112` deep charcoal base with `#D9AA70` warm champagne gold accent.
  * **Warm Ivory & Stone (Light)**: `#F2EFE8` paper base with `#A87543` warm cognac accent.
* **Filtered Technology Toolkit**: Interactive taxonomy across Languages, AI/ML, Data Science, Backend, Frontend, and Systems.
* **Accessible & Responsive**: Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and widescreen viewports with keyboard navigation and reduced-motion support.
